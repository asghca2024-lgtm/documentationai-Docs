## Overview

ASGHCA helps you move from interest to action with a clear preparation path. You start by choosing the exam track that matches your goal, then you connect with guidance, enroll in the right course, and begin your study routine.

This starter kit page is intentionally generic so you can customize it for your own program structure, support process, and learning materials. Use it as a practical launch point for first-time visitors who need a simple, confident next step.

<Callout kind="info">
  This page is a starter template. Replace the sample course names, contact details, and access steps with your own documentation and support process.
</Callout>

## Course Enrollment Steps

Follow a simple enrollment flow so you can move from research to study without losing momentum.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select your track" icon="check-circle">
    Choose the exam or course path that best fits your target license, timeline, and current preparation level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request consultation" icon="users">
    Share your background, preferred schedule, and preferred support channel so you can get a tailored recommendation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm enrollment" icon="download">
    Review the course outline, fee details, and start date before you finalize registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access study materials" icon="book-open">
    Log in to your learner space and download or view the assigned materials for your selected track.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Account or Consultation Setup

Use the option that fits your starting point. If you already know your track, go straight to enrollment. If you need help choosing, start with a consultation request.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Self-Enrollment" icon="zap">
    Create your learner profile, confirm your chosen track, and submit the required details in one pass. This works well when you already know which course you need.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Guided Consultation" icon="help-circle">
    Share your goals, current qualifications, and available study time. A support team can then suggest the most appropriate path before you enroll.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Document Review" icon="shield">
    Prepare basic identification and any relevant academic or licensing records so your registration process stays smooth and complete.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## First-Day Preparation Checklist

Before your first study session, make sure you are ready to focus. A short setup routine helps you avoid delays and keeps your first session productive.

| Item | What to prepare | Why it matters |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Goal | Define the exam or course outcome | Keeps your study plan focused |
| Time | Reserve a quiet study block | Helps you build a consistent habit |
| Materials | Open your assigned resources | Reduces setup time during the session |
| Notes | Keep one notebook or file ready | Makes revision and follow-up easier |

<Callout kind="tip">
  Keep your first session small and achievable. A 30-minute review is often better than trying to finish too much at once.
</Callout>

## What to Do Next

Once you have chosen a track and confirmed access, move into your first lesson, review the schedule, and set a weekly study rhythm. If you need help later, return to your consultation notes and adjust your path as needed.

<Expandable title="What should I do if I am not sure which course fits me?" default-open="true" title-type="p">
  
  Start with your destination exam, then compare it with your current qualification and timeline. If the match is not clear, request a consultation before enrolling.
  
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How can I keep my study routine consistent?" default-open="false" title-type="p">
  
  Pick the same study time each day, use one progress tracker, and review your next task before you close each session.
  
</Expandable>
