## Quick Start

1. Go to `https://dashboard.asghca.org`.
2. Click "Sign Up" and complete registration.
3. Verify your email.
4. Log in to access your documentation workspace.
5. Create or join a project to begin organizing docs.

## Tip

<Callout kind="tip">
Projects help you structure healthcare documentation for easy collaboration.
</Callout>
