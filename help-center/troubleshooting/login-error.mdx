---
title: Cannot Login to ASGHCA
description: Resolve login issues for ASGHCA dashboard.
---

## Issue
- Unable to log in
- "Invalid email or password" message

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check credentials">
    Ensure email and password are correct.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset password">
    Click “Forgot password?” on the login page and follow the instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear cache">
    Clear your browser cache and try again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">If the issue persists, contact support@asghca.org</Callout>
