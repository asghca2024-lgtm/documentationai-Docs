---
title: Create a New Documentation Project
description: How to add your first project in ASGHCA.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access dashboard">
    Go to `https://dashboard.asghca.org` and log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a project">
    Click "New Project" at the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill details">
    Enter your project name and optional description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click "Create". Your new project appears in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">You can invite collaborators to your project for team documentation.</Callout>
