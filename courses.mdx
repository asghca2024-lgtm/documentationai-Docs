### What each track usually covers

| Track group | Typical focus | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| OPRA preparation | Exam structure, clinical review, question practice | Learners targeting pharmacy registration outcomes |
| PEBC and NAPLEX | Core therapeutics, calculation practice, mock exams | Candidates preparing for North American pharmacy pathways |
| DHA, MOH, DOH, HAAD | Regulatory knowledge, local exam formats, revision plans | Learners pursuing Gulf-region licensing tracks |
| PSI, PBEC, Prometric | Syllabus mapping, timed practice, content review | Candidates needing exam-specific preparation support |
| IELTS and PTE | Speaking, writing, reading, and listening practice | Learners who need English language test readiness |

## Choosing the right path

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="OPRA" icon="book-open">
    
    OPRA-style preparation works best when you need a structured path with topic review, timed practice, and confidence-building mock tests.

    Focus on:

    - Core pharmacy concepts
    - Scenario-based questions
    - Revision checkpoints
    - Test-taking strategy
    
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="PEBC and NAPLEX" icon="shield">
    
    These tracks usually require deeper review of therapeutics, patient safety, calculations, and exam-style reasoning.

    Focus on:

    - Drug knowledge
    - Clinical decision making
    - Calculation accuracy
    - Repeated practice sets
    
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="DHA, MOH, DOH, HAAD" icon="settings">
    
    These programs often combine content review with local exam familiarity so you can study efficiently and avoid surprises.

    Focus on:

    - Regulatory topics
    - Local exam patterns
    - High-yield revision
    - Time management
    
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="IELTS and PTE" icon="users">
    
    Language support is useful when you need to improve communication skills alongside your professional exam preparation.

    Focus on:

    - Speaking fluency
    - Writing structure
    - Reading speed
    - Listening accuracy
    
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify your target exam" icon="target">
    Choose the exam or credential you want to pursue, then match it with the closest course track.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review prerequisites" icon="check-circle">
    Check whether you need prior education, language readiness, or document verification before you begin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your study format" icon="terminal">
    Decide whether you want live classes, recorded lessons, practice tests, or a blended schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a weekly plan" icon="calendar">
    Break your syllabus into manageable study blocks so you can track progress without overload.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example enrollment workflow

<CodeGroup tabs="JavaScript,Python,Bash">
  ```javascript
  const coursePlan = {
    examTrack: "DHA",
    level: "Intermediate",
    weeklyHours: 8,
    focusAreas: ["regulations", "practice tests", "revision"]
  };

  console.log(`Selected track: ${coursePlan.examTrack}`);
  ```

  ```python
  course_plan = {
      "exam_track": "DHA",
      "level": "Intermediate",
      "weekly_hours": 8,
      "focus_areas": ["regulations", "practice tests", "revision"]
  }

  print(f"Selected track: {course_plan['exam_track']}")
  ```

  ```bash
  export EXAM_TRACK="DHA"
  export WEEKLY_HOURS="8"
  echo "Selected track: ${EXAM_TRACK}"
  ```
